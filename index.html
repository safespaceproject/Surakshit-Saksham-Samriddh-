About SAFESCAPE 360°

Safety is a shared responsibility. Small improvements in public spaces can help people feel more informed, visible and supported.

Our Mission

Encourage safer and more accessible public spaces through awareness, community participation and practical safety recommendations.

  • Promote safer public-space planning
  • Encourage accessible safety information
  • Support community awareness and participation
  • Demonstrate how safety concerns could be organised

Our Proposed Solutions

Practical ideas that communities and responsible authorities can consider.

💡

Better Street Lighting

Identify poorly lit public areas and encourage maintenance and lighting improvements through the appropriate local channels.

🪧

Visible Safety Signage

Encourage clear signs that guide people to help points and display verified local emergency information.

🤝

Community Support

Promote awareness, inclusive access and collaboration among residents, community groups and relevant services.
